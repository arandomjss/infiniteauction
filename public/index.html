Game Rules

  • Gem shown each round: +1 to +4.
  • Submit one integer bid (≥ 0) per round.
  • Highest single bidder wins gem points; highest tie cancels gem but tied players still pay.
  • Lowest bidder(s) lose 1 point each.
  • Gravity Check every 4 rounds shows recent winning spend.
  • Final (The Punch): highest spender(s) eliminated; highest points among survivors wins.

Round flow

  1. Host starts the game (or next round automatically starts).
  2. A gem value is displayed and the timer begins.
  3. Active players place a single bid. If you don't bid before timer expires, your bid becomes 0.
  4. When all active players have bid or timer hits zero, bids are processed and results are shown.

Points & Spend

  • Winner gains +gemValue points and pays their bid (added to totalSpent).
  • Lowest bidder(s) lose 1 point.
  • High tie: no points awarded; tied players still pay.
  • Points public; totalSpent private per-round, revealed at The Punch.

Bids & Ties

  • One bid per round; integers ≥ 0.
  • No bid before timer → bid = 0.
  • Highest tie cancels the gem; tied players still pay their full bids.
  • Gravity pool increases by winner's bid (or highestBid × #tiedWinners on tie).

Bankruptcies

  1. The player(s) with the largest totalSpent among active players are marked bankrupt and eliminated.
  2. Bankrupted players cannot participate in further rounds and are shown as eliminated.
  3. If everyone is bankrupted there is no winner.

Gravity Check

  1. Triggered every 4 rounds.
  2. Displays total winning spend from the previous 4 rounds.
  3. Informational only; pool resets after display.

The Punch (final reveal)

  1. After the last round, players with the highest `totalSpent` become bankrupted (eliminated).
  2. Remaining players are compared by points; highest points wins.
  3. All players' `totalSpent` values are revealed publicly at this time.

Spectators

  1. Joining mid-game marks you as a spectator for the current round.
  2. Spectators cannot bid during that round.
  3. Spectators may participate starting the next round if not bankrupted.

Host Actions

  • Set `Total Rounds` and `Round Timer` before starting.
  • Start the game and end the current round early if needed.
  • Host disconnect → next connected player is promoted to host.

Example Round

  • Players: Alice, Bob, Carol, Dave.
  • Gem: +3.
  • Alice bids $10, Bob bids $10, Carol bids $2, Dave doesn't bid (0).
  • Highest tie (Alice & Bob): gem is cancelled; both pay $10 (added to their totalSpent).
  • Lowest bidder (Dave) loses 1 point for bidding 0.
  • Gravity pool gains $20 (10×2) for this round.
